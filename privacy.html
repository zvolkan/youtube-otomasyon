Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

YouTube Otomasyon is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses YouTube API Services.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, sets their titles, descriptions and publish times, and reads the channel's view and subscriber statistics.

How data is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to prepare a weekly performance report for the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only to keep the automation running. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third-party terms

By using this tool, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.
